# 1. Purpose

This document defines every user group and stakeholder that interacts with Docsflip. It establishes who the product serves before user journeys, features and requirements are defined.

---

# 2. Guiding Principle

Docsflip is designed around the needs of people and organisations that create, publish, distribute, manage or consume digital publications.

---

# 3. Primary User Groups

## 3.1 Independent Publisher
Creates and publishes their own content with minimal administration.

## 3.2 Organisation Publisher
Publishes on behalf of a company, NGO, institution or government body.

## 3.3 Organisation Administrator
Manages organisation settings, users and commercial controls.

## 3.4 Content Contributor
Prepares or uploads publication content but may not publish.

## 3.5 Publication Reader
Views and interacts with published digital publications.

---

# 4. Secondary Stakeholders

- Business owners

- Communications teams
- IT administrators
- Finance teams
- Support teams
- Platform operators

---

# 5. External Stakeholders

- Payment providers
- Technology partners
- Regulatory authorities
- Integration partners

---

# 6. User Goals

Typical goals include:

- Publish quickly.
- Share professionally.
- Understand commercial costs.
- Manage publications.
- Collaborate safely.
- Measure publication performance.

---

# 7. Needs by User Type

| User | Primary Need |
|------|--------------|
| Independent Publisher | Fast publishing |
| Organisation Publisher | Team publishing |
| Administrator | Governance & management |
| Reader | Easy access |
| Contributor | Content preparation |

# 9. Foundational Decisions

1. Users are defined before journeys.
2. Journeys are defined before features.
3. Features are defined before requirements.
4. Every requirement must trace to at least one user need.
