## 3. Key Architectural Outcomes

- **BIZ-001 now owns documented responsibility boundaries** (§2 — owns business strategy; does not own product identity, architecture domains, commercial mechanics, or user personas).
- **Complete constitutional traceability** — every value dimension, market position, and differentiator traces to CON-001 Philosophy and Principles.
- **Complete capability traceability** — §7 maps all 6 business capabilities to CAP-001 (L1), CAP-002 (L2/L3), and PA-001 domain sections.
- **Dependency integrity** — upstream dependencies (CON-001, CAP-001/002/004, PA-001) and downstream consumers (COM-001, USR-001, JNY-001, FEA-001, REQ-001) documented in §19.
- **Vocabulary aligned with PA-001 v0.4** — "Publications", "Organisations", capable-to-domain mapping throughout.

---

## 4. Lessons Learned

- **Planning-first approach:** Six analytical reports before implementation eliminated vocabulary conflicts and structural issues before content was written.
- **Controlled loop execution:** Three bounded loops with Founder gates at each stage prevented scope creep and ensured architectural consistency.
- **Traceability deferred to integration loop:** Keeping constitutional and capability traceability in Loop 3 prevented traceability from dictating content structure.
