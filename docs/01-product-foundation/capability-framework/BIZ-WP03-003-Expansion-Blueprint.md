# BIZ-WP03-003 — Business Expansion Blueprint

**Task:** DOCSFLIP-WP-03-PLAN  
**Date:** 2026-07-30

---

## 1. Proposed Structure

BIZ-001 should be organised into three thematic groups:

### Part 1 — Value Model

1. Business Purpose (from CON-001)
2. Value Creation (mapped to Publications)
3. Value Delivery (mapped to Reader Experience, Organisations)
4. Value Capture (mapped to Commercial)

### Part 2 — Market Model

5. Customer Segments (primary/secondary, mapped to USR-001)
6. Customer Relationships
7. Channels
8. Market Geography (Africa-first per CON-001)

### Part 3 — Operating Model

9. Key Capabilities (mapped to PA-001 domains + CAP-001)
10. Key Resources
11. Key Partnerships
12. Cost Structure
13. Revenue Streams (decomposed from value capture)
14. Strategic Differentiators
15. Business Model Decisions
