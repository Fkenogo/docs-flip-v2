# DOCSFLIP-CAP-004 --- Business Asset Model

**Version:** 0.1 (Founder Draft)\
**Repository Path:** `docs/01-product-foundation/capability-framework/`

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical business assets of Docsflip and
allocates a single authoritative capability owner for each asset.

## Design Principles

-   Every business asset has exactly one authoritative owner.
-   Multiple capabilities may use an asset, but only one governs it.
-   Technical data ownership is derived later in DAT-001.

# Ownership Rules

-   Ownership implies stewardship, governance and lifecycle
    responsibility.
-   Shared use does not imply shared ownership.
-   Ownership changes require Founder approval.

# Constitutional Statement

The Publication remains the primary business asset of Docsflip. All
other assets exist to create, govern, experience, commercialise or
measure publications.
