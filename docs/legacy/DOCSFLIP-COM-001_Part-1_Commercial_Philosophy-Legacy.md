## Part 1 — Commercial Philosophy

### Purpose

This document defines the commercial rules that govern how value moves through the Docsflip platform. It is the constitutional reference for pricing, credits, publications, hosting, subscriptions, wallets, renewals and payment behaviour.

It intentionally defines principles before numbers. Credit values, bundle sizes and pricing schedules will be specified in later commercial documents.

## Commercial Vision

Docsflip exists to make digital publishing accessible through a transparent, output-based commercial model.

> **Turn any PDF into a shareable digital publication from as little as $1. Publish when you need to. Pay only for what you use.**

## Commercial Objectives

- Affordable for occasional and frequent publishers.
- Predictable costs before publication.
- Support both individuals and organisations.
- Remove unnecessary subscription barriers.
- Provide sustainable platform revenue.
- Build trust through transparent credit usage.
- Scale across Africa.

## Core Commercial Principles

### CP-001 — Pay for Outputs

Credits are consumed only when a user receives a publication output.

### CP-002 — Transparency Before Consumption

Before credits are deducted, the user shall see:

- selected output;
- credit cost;
- total credits;
- remaining balance after approval.

### CP-003 — User Approval

Every intentional credit-consuming action requires explicit approval unless governed by an approved organisational workflow.

### CP-004 — Credits Represent Value

Customers purchase publishing capability, not software tiers.

### CP-005 — Publication-Centred Economy

The publication—not the credit—is the primary economic object.

### CP-006 — Purchased Credits Do Not Expire

Purchased credits remain owned by the customer. Promotional credits may follow different rules.

### CP-007 — Predictable Behaviour

Equivalent actions should always consume equivalent credits.

### CP-008 — Explainable Credit Usage

Every deduction shall be permanently traceable through a ledger.

### CP-009 — Separation of Ownership

Credit ownership, publication ownership and hosting status are separate concepts.

### CP-010 — Africa-First Design

The commercial architecture shall support progressive African payment integration while remaining globally compatible.

## Commercial Scope

This document will ultimately govern:

- credit economy;
- publication outputs;
- wallet behaviour;
- ledger rules;
- hosting economics;
- organisation allocations;
- promotional credits;
- subscriptions;
- refunds; and
- commercial lifecycle events.

## Guiding Rule

Whenever a commercial decision is proposed, ask:

> **What publication output does the customer receive?**

If there is no meaningful publication output, the action should normally not consume credits.
