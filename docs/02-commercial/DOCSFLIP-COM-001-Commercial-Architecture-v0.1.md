# 1. Purpose

This document defines the commercial operating model of Docsflip. It translates the business model into concrete commercial rules without describing technical implementation.

It is the authoritative source for how value is measured, charged, purchased and managed.

---

# 2. Commercial Philosophy

The commercial model is founded on five principles:

- Pay for publishing outcomes, not platform access.
- Keep pricing transparent and understandable.
- Allow organisations to publish when needed.
- Align revenue with customer value.
- Design for African purchasing realities while remaining globally applicable.

---

# 3. Commercial Model Overview

Docsflip operates on a **pay-as-you-publish** model.

Customers obtain publishing capacity through credits. Credits are consumed when publishing services are used rather than through mandatory recurring subscriptions.

---

# 4. Commercial Components

The commercial architecture comprises:

- Credit Economy
- Publication Output Model
- Wallet & Ledger
- Pricing Architecture
- Payment Strategy
- Organisation Accounts
- Hosting Lifecycle
- Renewal & Expiry Rules

Each component may be expanded into its own specialist document if required.

---

# 5. Credit Economy

Credits represent publishing capacity.

The credit system should:

- be transparent;
- be auditable;
- support individuals and organisations;
- avoid hidden consumption.

Detailed algorithms remain implementation concerns.

---

# 6. Publication Output Model

Commercial value is tied to publication outputs rather than platform access.

Publishing events become the principal commercial trigger.

---

# 7. Wallet & Ledger

The wallet records available publishing capacity.

The ledger provides an immutable history of:

- credit acquisition;
- credit consumption;
- adjustments;
- refunds (where applicable);
- administrative actions.

---

# 8. Pricing Architecture

Pricing should:

- remain simple;
- scale with customer usage;
- support different customer segments;
- avoid unnecessary complexity.

Specific price points are maintained separately from this document.

---

# 9. Payment Strategy

The platform should support payment methods appropriate to target markets, including African payment ecosystems and international options where appropriate.

Provider selection is an implementation decision.

---

# 10. Organisation Commercial Model

Commercial capabilities should support:

- individual users;
- organisations;
- multi-user accounts;
- delegated administration;
- shared commercial management.

---

# 11. Publication Hosting Lifecycle

The commercial model should define:

- publication activation;
- hosting duration;
- renewal;
- archival;
- retirement.

Technical implementation belongs elsewhere.

---

# 12. Commercial Governance Principles

The commercial architecture should always be:

- transparent;
- predictable;
- auditable;
- explainable;
- customer-centred.

---

# 14. Commercial Decisions

1. Publishing outcomes are the commercial unit of value.
2. Credits measure publishing capacity.
3. Pricing should remain transparent.
4. Commercial implementation must remain separable from technical implementation.
