## 3. Executive Summary

COM-001 v0.1 (L1 Skeleton) is **correctly positioned but materially incomplete**:

- **Purpose and architecture are sound.** The document correctly distinguishes commercial mechanics from business strategy and technical implementation. The dependency interpretation (architectural peer of BIZ-001/USR-001; content dependency on BIZ-001) is correctly embedded in the header.
- **Constitutional alignment is directional only.** All relevant CON-001 principles appear implicitly, but zero explicit PH/PP citations exist.
- **Capability alignment is partial (4/5).** Wallet, Credits, Payments, and Publication Outputs have skeleton coverage; **Entitlements is entirely missing**.
- **Business-model content dependency is rich but unmapped.** BIZ-001 §6/§8/§16 provide extensive commercial inputs that COM-001 must mechanically elaborate.
- **The 8 skeleton components all survive** and map to a coherent 9-part / 25-section target structure.
- **12 Founder decisions (FD-01..FD-12) are required** before Loop 2 (Content Expansion). The decisions are bounded, evidence-based, and each has options, consequences, and a recommendation — but the decisions must be made by the Founder.

## 6. Implementation Recommendation

**Use the established three-loop pattern:**

1. **Loop 1 — Structure Alignment** (v0.1 → v0.2): can proceed **immediately after Founder review** — requires no new commercial decisions. Restructure to the 25-section framework; add constitutional citations and ownership boundary.
2. **Founder Commercial Review Gate:** Founder reviews structure and provides decisions FD-01 through FD-12.
3. **Loop 2 — Commercial Content Expansion** (v0.2 → v0.3): requires all 12 decisions; expand ~138 content units.
4. **Founder Review Gate.**
5. **Loop 3 — Architectural Integration** (v0.3 → v0.4): full traceability, downstream consumers, governance, foundational decisions.
6. **Founder Approval + Closure:** MP-001 updated.

A different number of loops is **not supported by evidence** — COM-001 complexity is comparable to BIZ-001 (20 sections, 3 groups), successfully completed in three loops.
